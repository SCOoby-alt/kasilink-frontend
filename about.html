ABOUT KASILINK SA

Connecting Skills. Building Futures.

A South African digital service marketplace built to close the gap between skilled local plumbers and the customers who need them.

The problem we saw

Two groups of people, struggling to find each other.

Plumbers

Many independent plumbers and small plumbing businesses struggle to find customers and increase their visibility. Some plumbers spend significant time waiting for work or searching for customers instead of performing paid plumbing jobs.

Customers

Customers often struggle to identify suitable and trustworthy plumbers, especially when they experience plumbing emergencies or maintenance problems.

Our solution

KasiLink SA brings plumbers and customers together through one digital platform.

What plumbers can do on KasiLink SA

  • Create a professional profile.
  • Display services, skills, experience and qualifications.
  • Show previous work.
  • Receive customer ratings and reviews.
  • Receive enquiries, quotation requests and bookings.

What customers can do on KasiLink SA

  • Search for plumbers by location or required service.
  • Compare plumbers and view their profiles.
  • View ratings and reviews.
  • Request quotations and book plumbing services.
  • Identify plumbers for emergency plumbing work.

Our mission

KasiLink SA exists to give skilled local plumbers real visibility, and to give customers a trustworthy, simple way to find them — turning waiting time into working time, and guesswork into informed decisions.

Trust

Verified profiles, honest reviews and transparent pricing so customers can hire with confidence.

Local Skills

Championing township and community-based plumbers whose skills often go unseen by the wider market.

Reliability

Fast access to plumbers for both routine maintenance and urgent, emergency plumbing needs.

About this platform

KasiLink SA is a South African plumbing services marketplace connecting customers with local, skilled plumbers. The six plumber profiles currently listed are sample business records used to demonstrate how search, booking and ratings work on the platform.
