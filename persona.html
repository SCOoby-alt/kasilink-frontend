TN

Thabo Nkosi

Customer Persona

Age
34
Location
Soshanguve, Pretoria
Occupation
Retail store supervisor
Household
Owns a 3-bedroom home, lives with family
Devices
Uses a smartphone for most online tasks

Goals

  • Find a trustworthy plumber quickly during an emergency.
  • Compare prices before committing to a booking.
  • Avoid being overcharged or let down by unreliable workers.

Frustrations

  • Relies on word-of-mouth referrals that are not always available.
  • Struggles to judge a plumber's skill level before hiring.
  • Has been let down by no-shows in the past.

How KasiLink SA helps

Thabo can search by location and service, compare plumber ratings and reviews, and request a quotation or booking directly through the platform — without relying on guesswork.

SD

Sipho Dlamini

Plumber Persona

Age
29
Location
Mamelodi, Pretoria
Occupation
Independent, self-employed plumber
Experience
6 years in residential plumbing
Devices
Uses a smartphone; limited access to a laptop

Goals

  • Get a steady stream of paid work without constant marketing effort.
  • Build a visible track record of completed jobs and reviews.
  • Spend more time working and less time searching for customers.

Frustrations

  • Limited visibility beyond his immediate neighbourhood.
  • No easy way to showcase past work or qualifications online.
  • Inconsistent income between jobs.

How KasiLink SA helps

Sipho can create a profile showing his skills, experience and previous work, receive enquiries and bookings directly, and build a reputation through customer ratings — increasing his visibility beyond word-of-mouth.
