How KasiLink SA works

A simple four-step process for customers and plumbers.

1

Customer Need

Customer needs plumbing services in their home, office or project.

2

Search & Connect

Customer searches on KasiLink SA and connects with available plumbers.

3

Service Delivery

The plumber provides the required plumbing service as agreed.

4

Feedback & Review

The customer gives feedback and rates the service so other customers can make informed decisions.

Benefits

KasiLink SA is built to work for both sides of the marketplace.

For Customers

  • Easily find suitable plumbers.
  • Compare plumbing services.
  • View plumber profiles.
  • Read ratings and reviews.
  • Request quotations.
  • Book services.
  • Make more informed decisions before hiring.

For Plumbers

  • Increase customer visibility.
  • Showcase skills and experience.
  • Display qualifications.
  • Show previous projects.
  • Receive more enquiries.
  • Build a professional reputation.
  • Increase opportunities for paid work.
